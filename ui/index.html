INCOME PERSPECTIVE

Estimate your dividend income.

Actual distribution data

Dividend estimates use actual reported trailing 12-month distributions and the latest available share price. Missing data is shown as unavailable; no sample yields are used. Each estimate assumes the full investment goes into that fund.

CAD and USD are supported scenario currencies. Changing currency keeps the number you entered; it does not convert it. The same currency applies to the growth scenario. Each fund’s trading currency is shown in the comparison. Estimates in a different currency assume a constant exchange rate and exclude conversion costs.

Estimated annual income = investment amount × actual trailing 12-month distributions per share ÷ latest available share price. This uses fractional shares and past distributions; future payouts can change. This amount is separate from the growth scenario starting investment. Monthly equivalents are averages, not payment schedules. Horizon income applies the current trailing distribution yield to the projected end balance; it is a run rate, not cumulative dividends. These pre-tax estimates exclude withholding and currency conversion. Distributions vary and are not guaranteed. The growth chart assumes total return with dividends reinvested: do not add this income to its balance. About fund distributions

Sources & calculation methodology

Expense ratios are a static issuer-source snapshot checked November 18, 2026. They do not refresh automatically. Source links appear in the comparison. Fund fees can change. The saved list can be expanded through Alpha Vantage search. Provider-supported CAD/USD ETFs and split-share companies identified by their names are returned; coverage is not universal. Provider results are cached on the server for 24 hours (search: seven days). Retrieval time is not the quote date. When available, trailing distribution yield is the sum of the past year’s distributions by ex-date divided by the latest available quote; otherwise the dividend estimate is unavailable. Prices and distributions can be delayed. Public Yahoo Finance data supplements Alpha Vantage where dividend history is missing; source links are shown per fund. Some distributions can include interest, capital gains or return of capital. The app limits itself to 23 provider requests daily in UTC; usage outside this app also counts against the provider’s allowance.

Monthly compounding uses (1 + gross annual return − annual expense ratio)^(1/12) − 1, with contributions at each month-end. Fee drag is the difference from an otherwise identical no-fee scenario, including foregone growth; it is not a statement of actual fees paid. Excludes taxes, inflation, commissions, spreads, currency conversion and tracking differences. Assumptions are illustrative and not investment advice. Selections and inputs are saved only in this browser.
